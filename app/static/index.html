Choose the image pages you want to check

Add up to 5 screenshots or photos from the same email, message, or document, in reading order. When you press Check these images, they are sent securely to Google Gemini on Vertex AI for this check. Before You Tap does not intentionally store them.

Choose the audio message you want to check

Choose a saved voicemail, voice message, or audio file. When you press Check this audio message, it is sent securely to Google Gemini on Vertex AI for this check. Before You Tap does not intentionally store it. The app never listens to a live call or turns on your microphone.
